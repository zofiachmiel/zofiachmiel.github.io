In Country Project Manager

Rural School Construction — Kaihura, Uganda

Kaihura, Uganda · Sep 2023 — Jul 2024

As In Country Project Manager, I led an 11-student team through an 8-week rural school construction project — managing daily operations, coordinating material logistics, and communicating with local laborers to keep the build aligned with design specifications, schedule goals, and safety protocols.

Working on the ground meant adapting constantly — material shortages and delivery delays were a regular reality, and every setback meant re-sequencing work or sourcing alternatives without losing sight of the project's design specs, timeline, or the needs of the community the school was being built for.

The project came together on time and under budget, and gave me an early, hands-on lesson in what it actually takes to deliver infrastructure in a resource-constrained environment: clear communication, constant problem-solving, and a team that trusts each other to adapt when the plan changes.
