FaunaTag — Wearable Biometric Tag for Whale Health Monitoring

Durham, NC · Jan 2026 — Present

FaunaTag is a biometric wearable used to monitor whale health in the field, currently supporting ongoing research on whales off the coast of Brazil. As a Hardware Engineering Intern at FaunaLabs, I'm contributing to its mechanical redesign, using CAD to improve the tag's shape, durability, waterproofing, and deployment reliability for long-term, field-ready physiological data collection in harsh marine environments.

I'm collaborating with a multidisciplinary team to integrate sensors and optimize the device's form factor for physiological tracking in wildlife applications — balancing hydrodynamics, attachment reliability, and internal component layout.

Throughout this work I'm applying principles of biomedical device design, waterproofing, and mechanical resilience to improve FaunaTag's functionality and reliability for deployment in marine mammal research, directly supporting field studies and data collection efforts.
