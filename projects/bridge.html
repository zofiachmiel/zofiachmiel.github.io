Project Manager

Suspended Pedestrian Bridge & WASH System — Eswatini

Durham, NC & Eswatini · Aug 2024 — Sep 2025

I managed the design and planning of a suspended pedestrian bridge and WASH (water, sanitation, and hygiene) system serving 300+ residents in Eswatini, overseeing stakeholder coordination and ensuring alignment with engineering standards, environmental constraints, and long-term functionality goals.

Working remotely, I collaborated with engineers, NGOs, and local officials to develop structural plans, perform site feasibility assessments, and ensure safe, sustainable delivery of the bridge and WASH infrastructure — balancing technical requirements against the realities of a remote, resource-constrained build site.

I applied systems thinking and risk assessment throughout to improve infrastructure resilience and help ensure year-round access to water and safe pedestrian transportation for the community.
