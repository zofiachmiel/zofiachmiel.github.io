Bioreactor System — Plastic Bioremediation Research

Durham, NC · Jan 2026 — Present

As Mechanical Design Lead on a plastic bioremediation and toxicity research project, I'm designing and prototyping a bioreactor system used to test plastic-degrading enzymes and study the toxicity of plastic additives in lab settings.

The system integrates biological and mechanical components, including cell culture handling and fluid control design — which means balancing sterility, flow control, and ease of monitoring within a compact lab-scale rig.

The broader goal is to apply mechanical design toward scalable tools that address plastic bioaccumulation through marine bioremediation systems, connecting hands-on prototyping to environmental research.
