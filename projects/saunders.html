Saunders Construction · Estimator Intern

Preconstruction Estimating — Gymnastics Training Facility

Denver, CO · May 2025 — Aug 2025

As an Estimator Intern at Saunders Construction, I led preconstruction for an approximately $8M gymnastics training facility, managing cost estimation, trade coordination, and risk mitigation across civil, structural, and architectural scopes to ensure alignment with budget targets, technical requirements, timelines, and feasibility from concept to pricing.

I created detailed bid tabs, cost models, and scope matrices using Excel and Bluebeam to support pricing accuracy, subcontractor alignment, and constructability review — work that fed directly into risk mitigation, competitive bidding, and decision-making during the preconstruction process.

I also presented value engineering recommendations and collaborated with engineers to align design decisions with technical, budget, and schedule requirements — an early lesson in designing for manufacturability and cost from the earliest stages of a project, a mindset I carry directly into product design work.
