Vacuum Pump Reliability Solution

May 2026 — Aug 2026

As a Global Facilities & Engineering Intern at Boehringer Ingelheim, I was assigned to a vacuum pump system that supplies several GMP and FDA-regulated process rooms and spaces in the process development lab.

The pump's return piping was building up sediment far faster than expected — pipes carrying water that gets heated to extreme temperatures by the vacuum pump itself. I ran a series of tests and brought in an outside vendor to help diagnose the issue, and we traced it back to the sediment being almost entirely calcium: the incoming city water was extremely hard, with a high pH that was accelerating scale formation.

From there, I designed a water softener system to remove hardness before it ever reached the pump, working through the system design and coordinating with outside vendors to source and install the equipment. It was a good example of working backward from a physical symptom to its root chemistry, then building a fix that addressed the actual cause instead of just the downstream damage.
