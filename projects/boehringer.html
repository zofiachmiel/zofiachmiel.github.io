Boehringer Ingelheim · Global Facilities & Engineering Intern

Equipment Disposal Workflow App

May 2026 — Aug 2026

As a Global Facilities & Engineering Intern at Boehringer Ingelheim, I noticed that the process for disposing of equipment across the site was undocumented and spread across multiple approval groups — making it hard for anyone to follow consistently or track where a request actually stood.

I documented the existing process end-to-end and created workflow diagrams and process maps to standardize it and make it visible to everyone involved, from facilities staff to the teams responsible for sign-off.

From there, I designed and built an HTML-based prototype for a future equipment disposal app, translating the mapped-out workflow into an interface meant to guide users through each step and make the process self-explanatory.

The prototype gave the facilities team a concrete starting point for building out a real internal tool, and gave me hands-on experience turning a messy, undocumented process into something structured, visual, and easy to use — a small-scale but complete product design exercise inside a large pharmaceutical operation.
